Consent to the taking and use of photographs and audiovisual recordings

I give PRO.MED.CS Praha a.s. my consent to take, through an authorised photographer, videographer or other service provider, photographs and audiovisual recordings in which I may appear during the PRO.MED.CS Christmas Party and to subsequently use and disseminate my likeness captured in such recordings for the purposes of internal and external communication, corporate presentation and employer branding, in particular through the Company's website and social media channels, annual report and internal communication and presentation materials.

This permission is granted pursuant to Sections 84 et seq. of Act No. 89/2012 Coll., the Civil Code, as amended.

To the extent that the photographs or audiovisual recordings contain my personal data, I also consent to their processing by PRO.MED.CS Praha a.s. for the purposes specified above.

Giving this permission and consent is entirely voluntary, and neither refusal to provide it nor its subsequent withdrawal will have any effect on my ability to attend the Christmas Party, nor on my employment, working conditions, remuneration or any other standing within the Company. I may withdraw my permission and consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal; withdrawal also does not affect photographs and audiovisual recordings that have already been published or disseminated in accordance with this permission, although the Company will, following withdrawal, make reasonable efforts to cease its own further use of them.

Permission and consent may be withdrawn by contacting communications@promedcs.com.

Information regarding the processing of personal data, including information on data subject rights and on the right to lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection, is available in the applicable privacy notice.
